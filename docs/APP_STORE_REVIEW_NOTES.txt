Unwrapt — App Review Notes

WHAT THE APP DOES
Unwrapt is a personal gifting assistant. Users add the people they care about, optionally connect Google Calendar to import birthdays and anniversaries, and subscribe to VIP so Thea (our agent) can watch occasions and recommend gifts.

DEMO LOGIN
Sign in with Google using the reviewer account we provide in App Store Connect Notes (or request via App Review reply). After sign-in, complete or skip onboarding as needed. VIP can be purchased with a Sandbox Apple ID.

SUBSCRIPTIONS (IAP)
Digital VIP membership is sold only via Apple In-App Purchase:
  Product ID: unwrapt_vip_monthly
  Type: Auto-renewable monthly subscription
  Price: $4.99/month
Managed through RevenueCat + StoreKit. “Restore purchases” is available on the paywall.

PHYSICAL GOODS
When ordering/shipping a physical gift, payment uses Stripe. That charge is for a physical product, not digital content. VIP access itself is IAP-only inside the iOS app.

CALENDAR
Optional. Connect Google Calendar to import birthdays/anniversaries. We request calendar-related access only for that purpose.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings → Account includes a delete-account request flow.
